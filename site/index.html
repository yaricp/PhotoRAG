PhotoRAG

Your photo library, described and searchable by AI — private by default, and fully under your control.

Download latest release View source on GitHub

About

  • AI descriptions
    Every photo gets a natural-language description from a vision model.
  • Text extraction (OCR)
    Finds and indexes text that appears inside your photos.
  • Semantic search
    Search your library by meaning, not just filenames or tags.
  • Duplicate detection
    Finds exact and near-duplicate photos automatically.
  • Private by default
    Descriptions, tags, and search all run on your machine out of the box — your photos never leave your computer unless you explicitly choose otherwise.
  • Your choice of AI
    Every AI capability (descriptions, tagging, search, translation, chat) can be switched independently between a local model and a remote provider you configure yourself (OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama, and more) — you decide where your data goes.

Getting Started

  1. 1. Install

    Download the file for your platform above, then open it. On macOS, if you see a warning that the app is from an unidentified developer, right-click the app and choose Open.

    Full installation walkthrough
  2. 2. First launch — Setup Wizard

    On first launch, the Setup Wizard walks you through choosing which AI models to install. You can skip optional models and download them later from Settings.

  3. 3. Configure Settings and Models

    Open Settings to set your language and default photo folder. Open Models to choose which AI capabilities are active — descriptions, tagging, search, translation, and OCR.

  4. 4. Add your photos: Watcher or Scan

    On the Folders page, add a Watcher to automatically process new photos as they arrive in a folder (ideal for syncing from your phone), or run a one-time Scan to process an existing folder of photos.

  5. 5. See the results

    Track progress on the Processing page, then browse your described, searchable library in the Gallery.
